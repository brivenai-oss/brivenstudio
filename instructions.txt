To finish the setup:

chatbot.js → upload to your site's root, same level as index.html
_middleware.js → upload into a functions folder at your project root 
(you've already got this part done)
Remove the old <div id="ccw-root-briven-studio"> + 
<script> block from every page it's currently pasted into
Upload everything together and redeploy

One thing worth knowing for later: 
your actual widget config (business info, pricing, webhook URLs) now lives inside chatbot.js, 
not spread across five HTML files. So next time you want to update anything, 
either edit this file directly, or regenerate a fresh snippet from chatbot_generator.html 
and swap the CONFIG block in — just keep the root-creation bit near the top 
(the part that creates its own <div>) since that's specific to this standalone-file setup, 
not something the Generator outputs by default.
